# GNSS-based Time synchronisation of Stereo Images

**Laure-Anne Gueguen, Martin Wieser, Gottfried Mandlburger.** *ISPRS Archives*, XLVIII-G-2025, 535–540 (2025). [DOI and official full text](https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-archives-XLVIII-G-2025-535-2025)

**Read status:** Full six-page peer-reviewed conference contribution checked; metadata verified on the ISPRS publisher page.

## Content checked

Two Sony ILX-LR1 cameras use GNSS timing and a 3×7 LED board to assess trigger-to-exposure latency. The authors report latency below 2.5 ms and, after real-time correction, 470 μs RMSE in inter-camera exposure-time difference. The discussion identifies clock drift during longer GNSS outages as a remaining issue.

## Relation to this project

This is a direct comparison for [[Workstation - Camera clock drift]]. It measures trigger/exposure timing with GNSS and LEDs; the SASL report estimates the relative rate of RTCP/NTP and RTP clocks during a free-running 30-minute observation. The SASL report does not establish actual exposure skew.
